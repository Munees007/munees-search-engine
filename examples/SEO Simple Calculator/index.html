Free Simple Online Calculator

Enter an expression with your keyboard or use the calculator keys.

How to use this simple calculator

Type a math expression into the display, then press Enter to calculate it. You can also select the on-screen keys. Use C to remove the last character, or AC to clear the entire expression and start again.

Basic math operations

This free online calculator supports addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, and percentages. Use parentheses to group parts of an expression, such as (8 + 2) * 3. You can enter numbers and operators from your keyboard, use Backspace to edit, or press Delete to clear the display.
